About Us

Welcome to Mind & Learning Foundery

Mind & Learning Foundery was founded by Priyanka, a QCI-Certified Yoga Instructor with a deep passion for holistic education and personal growth. A postgraduate from Mumbai University (2010), she is currently completing her Diploma in Counselling (Child & Adult).

From an early age, yoga and meditation transformed her life — teaching her the power of a calm mind and a strong body in today's fast-paced world. That realisation inspired her to build a space where strong academics meet emotional well-being.

"Strong academics are essential for succeeding in a competitive world — but true potential blooms when nurtured with a balanced, stress-free mind and body."

Every child at the Foundery receives caring, encouraging guidance built to develop rock-solid concepts, unshakable confidence, and a positive mindset — combining expert academic teaching with counselling, yoga, meditation, and laughter yoga.

Our Approach

Four elements, one integrated method.

Academics and wellbeing aren't separated into different rooms here — they're cast together, so children build focus, confidence and calm alongside strong concepts.

Academics

Concept-first coaching in Maths & Science with regular practice, worksheets and doubt-solving.

Counselling

One-on-one guidance for children and adults to manage emotions and build resilience.

Yoga & Meditation

Breathing, posture and stillness practice that sharpens focus and lowers stress.

Laughter Yoga

Playful, positive-energy sessions that build a light, joyful relationship with learning.
