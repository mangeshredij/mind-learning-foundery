About us

Welcome to Mind & Learning Foundery

Mind & Learning Foundery was founded by Priyanka, a QCI-certified yoga instructor with a deep passion for holistic education and personal growth. She is a Mumbai University postgraduate (2010) and is completing her Diploma in Counselling (Child & Adult).

Yoga and meditation changed her life from an early age. They taught her the power of a calm mind and a strong body, and inspired her to build a space where strong academics meet emotional well-being.

Strong academics are essential for succeeding in a competitive world, but true potential blooms when nurtured with a balanced, stress-free mind and body.

Every child receives caring, encouraging guidance that builds rock-solid concepts, confidence and a positive mindset, through expert teaching, counselling, yoga, meditation and laughter yoga.
